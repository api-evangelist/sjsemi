# SJSemi

> SJSemiconductor (Jiangyin) Corp. — a Chinese Middle-End-Of-Line (MEOL) pure-play
> semiconductor foundry and OSAT provider founded in 2014 as a JCET/SMIC joint venture.
> SJSemi manufactures: 12-inch wafer bumping, Cu pillar and RDL, wafer-level chip-scale
> packaging, chip probing and final test, and advanced 3D multi-die integration including
> fan-out, TSV and silicon-interposer 2.5D packaging. Its customers are chip designers
> in smartphone, 5G, high-performance computing, data-center and automotive markets.

## Important for agents

SJSemi publishes **no public API, no developer portal, and no machine-readable interface
contract**. It sells physical manufacturing services under commercial supply agreements,
not software. As of 2026-08-28 there is no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, MCP server,
A2A agent card, `/llms.txt`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, or any `/.well-known/` document
served from any SJSemi host. Do not attempt to construct or call an SJSemi API endpoint;
none is known to exist. Engagement with SJSemi is a sales and supply-chain conversation
conducted through its contact page.
